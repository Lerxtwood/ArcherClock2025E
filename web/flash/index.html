Leave “Erase device” unchecked to keep Wi-Fi credentials, themes, and display settings. Select it for a fresh start that clears all saved settings.

01 / CONNECT

Plug in over USB.

Use a data cable and close serial monitors. If connection stalls, hold BOOT while connecting, then release it.

02 / INSTALL

Choose your release.

Select a version, click Connect & install, and choose the clock’s serial port. Keep it plugged in until installation completes.

03 / CONFIGURE

Make it yours.

After a fresh install, join ArcherClock-XXXXXX Wi-Fi and open 192.168.4.1. Save your network, then tune your themes at archer-clock.local.
